# BarkPark

**Find your pack.** BarkPark is a web app for dog owners: register the dogs you
bring to your favorite dog parks, see every dog park near you on a map, and
check in when you arrive so friends know who's at the park.

## What it does

- **Dog registration** — owners create a profile for each of their dogs: name,
  breed, size, photo, temperament tags ("ball-obsessed", "shy with big dogs").
- **Park map** — an interactive map centered on the user's location showing
  nearby dog parks as paw-print pins. Tapping a pin opens a park detail card:
  amenities (fenced, off-leash hours, water station, small-dog area, shade),
  distance, and who's checked in now.
- **Check-in** — one tap when you arrive with your dog. Park pages show live
  activity ("4 dogs here now").
- **Preferred parks** — favorite the parks you visit most; they surface first.
- **Sponsored vet locations** *(new)* — the map also shows nearby veterinarians
  who pay for placement. Vet pins are visually distinct (cross/clinic icon,
  subtle "Sponsored" badge) and open a card with the clinic's hours, services,
  and a call button. Sponsorships fund the app — no subscription for owners.

## The product in one scene

A user opens BarkPark on their phone's browser. A friendly map fills the
screen — soft green park areas, warm paw-print pins, their own location pulsing
in the middle. A bottom sheet lists "Your parks" with photos of their two dogs
ready to check in. One pin away, a small teal cross marks "Maple Vet Clinic —
Sponsored". Everything feels like a sunny Saturday morning at the park.

## Visual identity

- **Tone**: warm, playful, trustworthy — a neighborhood app, not a corporate one
- **Palette**: grass green and warm amber primaries, cream background, a teal
  accent reserved for vet/sponsored elements
- **Iconography**: rounded paw-print pins for parks, teal cross pins for vets,
  circular dog avatars
- **Typography**: rounded, friendly sans-serif
- **Mascot energy**: happy medium-size mutt, tongue out, slightly goofy

## Tech (for context, not the image)

Svelte web UI · Go backend · Postgres/PostGIS geo search · built by three
teams (Design, Backend, Frontend) on a six-week release cycle.
